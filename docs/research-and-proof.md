# Research makes the thesis testable and the case study defensible

This is a research protocol, not a report of new findings. Existing customer research, competitor audit and presentation artifacts should be linked as they are located. The [thread register](programme-register.md) supplies ownership IDs.

## Future of Commerce

The project asks what an online store needs to do when discovery, comparison, purchase and care can happen across a storefront, a social feed, a spoken interface and an assistant. The gated Shopify storefront is the working proof of concept. Site-of-the-Month-calibre craft and usability are the design benchmark. The [grand plan](awwwards-grand-plan.md) tracks further internal design ambitions.

**Positioning hypothesis.** For frequent home cooks choosing a long-lived piece after a change of kitchen, showing hob compatibility, dimensions and care at the point of comparison will increase correct product selections because they need to know a pan will work in the new kitchen before they commit to keeping it. “Live with cookware that outlasts you” gives that behaviour a reason to matter beyond the first purchase.

| Workstream | Question or decision |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose and scope | Can the same piece remain intelligible from discovery through handover? Compare the existing browse journey to assisted routes. Treat paid orders and fulfilment as a later study. |
| Customer and market | Begin with the moving cook. Compare gifting and professional replacement tasks against dated evidence and competing ways to solve them. |
| Offer and economics | Test willingness to pay for the piece, useful accessories and care. Model margin after maker pay, packaging, delivery, returns and support. |
| Brand and trust | Explain longevity through care and repairability. Tie technical and provenance claims to the evidence ledger. Label fictional testimonials at the point of reading. |
| Journey and pages | Trace social and voice discovery to product details, Finder, a suitable selection or an explicit no-fit answer, and Kansa for care. |
| Content and acquisition | Compare a maker-led social demonstration with a product-led demonstration. Give both a direct piece link and the same product facts. |
| Measurement and KPIs | Use fit accuracy and false-positive recommendations as the first decision metrics. Establish a baseline before comparing paths. |
| Privacy, ethics and accessibility | Make care accessible without an account. Test keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion and text equivalents for voice and heat maps. Collect only the data needed for the study. |
| Operations and service | Verify part fit, instructions, maker responsibilities and ownership of record updates before promising a repair or fulfilment service. |
| Test, learn and revise | Run the same tasks on baseline and assisted versions. Record failures and reasons, change the underlying spec or journey, then rerun. |

## Two questions about the proposed artisan programme

Rochet and Tirole ask how a platform gets both sides to participate and how it allocates charges between them. Test price sensitivity on each side, the value more cooks bring to makers and more makers bring to cooks, and the alternatives available when makers can sell through several channels. That model makes price structure, cross-side effects and steering explicit. Work, data ownership and bargaining power require additional evidence. [Platform Competition in Two-Sided Markets (2003)](https://doi.org/10.1162/154247603322493212)

Srnicek asks how platforms turn intermediation into lasting economic power through infrastructure, data and control. This lens helps test whether a subsidy later buys dependence, who can export a maker's catalogue and how commissions, ranking and access might shape artisans' work. The book offers a political economy of platform power. The fee allocation still needs a demand and cost model. Maison Tavo's working response is non-exclusive participation, visible maker credit, portable records and care access without a customer account. [Platform Capitalism (2017)](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Platform%2BCapitalism-p-9781509504879)

## Customer picture and assumption log

The founder's move supplies one observed need. The [September 2026 competitor audit](audit-machine-readability.md) supplies a dated snapshot of public product information. The three storefront voices are fictional illustrations of buying situations.

| Situation and desired progress | Barrier to test | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A frequent cook moves to induction and wants one pan ready for weeknight use. | It is difficult to tell whether the base, diameter and care fit the new hob and routine. | Interview cooks who recently moved or changed hobs. Observe compatibility queries, reviews and comparison behaviour. Compare incumbent product pages and prices. |
| A wedding guest wants to give a vessel the recipient will cook with. | The guest cannot confidently judge provenance, first-use care or the work the gift imposes. | Interview gift buyers and recipients. Observe gifting occasions and review questions. Compare cookware gifts with accessible substitutes and budgets. |
| A small kitchen needs to replace several heavily used pans. | Weight, usable diameter and parts availability are hard to compare before ordering. | Interview operators using their existing purchasing checklists. Observe replacement and support questions. Benchmark supplier lead times, prices and part listings. |

Record each assumption with its source, date, confidence and a task that could disprove it. Collect customer voices, observed behaviour and market context independently, including cases where a buyer chooses a cheaper or simpler substitute.

## One evidence ledger serves claims, product data and presentations

Each entry records ID, claim/question, evidence tier, source URL/file, author/date, observation date, quotation or paraphrase, method, limitations, consent/licence where applicable, affected SKUs/surfaces and review status. Material claims need conditions and units. Narrative inspiration does not verify manufacturing origin.

Keep the verified origin diagnostic separate from market prevalence. Keep competitor observations separate from private-backend assumptions. Keep user-reported project history attributed until the original artifact is archived. The literature site, About page, memo and pitch should cite the same entry IDs.

## Discoverability is measured with a repeatable protocol

Record a baseline before new public publication where still possible; if the brand is already public, call it the earliest recorded baseline. Use stable question families: brand identity; product existence; stove fit; care; comparisons; recommendation; purchase/negotiation. Include English, Hindi/Hinglish and spoken/transcribed variants.

For each run log model/service and version if visible, date, locale, browsing state, conversation reset state, exact prompt, response, cited sources and scorer. Score correct identity, grounded facts, unknowns handled, fabricated claims and actionable links separately. Save failures rather than rerunning until a flattering answer appears.

Repeat on a declared cadence and after meaningful publications. “Time to be known” means time to first observed correct response under this protocol, not proven indexing latency. A single response cannot establish market-wide visibility or attribute improvement to one intervention. The living About page uses dated observed figures with denominators.

## Netnography turns real confusion into benchmark tasks

Collect public forum questions and how-to-choose-a-pan sources with URLs, dates and context. Group by stove compatibility, task, maintenance tolerance, weight/handling, material trust, price and gifting. Remove personal identifiers where unnecessary. Preserve the distinction between sourced questions and synthetic variants.

Version TavaBench with canonical questions, acceptable evidence, scoring rules and known ambiguity. Include no-fit and insufficient-information questions. Publish permitted excerpts or paraphrases rather than copying entire discussions. Monthly runs are a planned cadence, not an already operating automation.

## Competitor benchmarking tests the actual wedge

Start with Made In, then the named competitor set. Inspect visible product explanation, finder questions, navigation, care, price ladder and public structured data. Record what an external reader can actually retrieve and parse. A missing field in one inspected response does not prove the brand has no structured backend.

The proposed differentiator is deeper, consistent product meaning across interfaces. Avoid “first in cookware” until a sufficiently defined and current audit supports a narrower statement. The existing machine-readability audit is a dated input, not permanent proof.

## Demand feedback uses consistent questions and honest denominators

Prepare three core questions: what is unclear or hard about choosing this tool; which information changes confidence in fit; what next action would the participant actually take and why? Follow up with trade-offs and willingness to pay without treating a compliment as demand.

Tag recruitment source, prior relationship, experience and relevant kitchen context. Friends/family, chefs, home cooks and enthusiast forums are different samples. Use consent, anonymized notes and community rules. Track invitations, visits, responses, qualified interest and waitlist actions separately. Outreach requires an explicit sending instruction; this document only prepares the plan.

## The live-room study is a demonstration with sampling limits

Use a printed care card and verified QR, invite peers to ask their own assistants a controlled question, and tally correct, incomplete, fabricated and unknown responses. Record prompt/model differences and avoid collecting private account content. Present it as a convenience-sample study, not population validation.

## Measurement tests usefulness as well as attention

Define events and denominators before reading outcomes: finder completion; accepted recommendation; unresolved question; product-to-cart; care-kit attachment; waitlist conversion; referral/gift intent; discoverability correctness. Compare assisted and unassisted journeys only with clear task and sample controls. Self-selection and small samples limit causal claims.

| KPI | Definition | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct fit rate | Correct shortlists or correct no-fit answers ÷ scored tasks. Record unsafe compatibility assertions separately. | Could people or assistants choose the right piece? |
| Grounded answer rate | Assistant answers with accurate, traceable material, fit or care facts ÷ scored prompts. | Can a cook use and verify the answer? |
| Finder completion | Completed recommendations, including no-fit, ÷ Finder starts. | Could visitors complete the comparison? |
| Identifier-to-care success | Timed attempts that reach the correct care record via scan or typed code ÷ attempts. Measure once a prototype identifier exists. | Did the instructions travel with the piece? |
| Conversion rate | Eligible sessions with the declared action ÷ eligible sessions. Use a clearly defined demo action today and report paid conversion separately when real orders exist. | Did visitors complete the intended action? |
| Average order value | Net merchandise revenue after discounts and returns, excluding tax and shipping, ÷ placed paid orders in the same cohort. | How much merchandise revenue did each real order generate? |
| Customer acquisition cost | Acquisition spend for a defined cohort ÷ first-time paying customers attributed to that cohort. | What did each new paying customer cost under the stated credit rule? |

Only compute order value and acquisition cost once the store has real revenue and acquisition data. Platform attribution assigns credit by a rule. Use controlled comparisons or an incrementality test before claiming that a channel caused the outcome.

Set decision thresholds after establishing feasible traffic and baseline, before outcome interpretation. If assistance adds friction without improving comprehension or task completion, revise the guidance hypothesis. If structured projections disagree, fix the source system before adding more generated answers. Revenue and conversion improvements remain hypotheses until measured.

## The final narrative is selective while the register remains complete

Every memo/video item needs one sentence explaining how it proves find, understand, negotiate or reverence for the tool. Include the strongest traceable evidence; leave enabling work and unsupported ideas in the register. A hostile-marker review logs objections, fixes and unresolved disagreements rather than outsourcing judgment to a model.
