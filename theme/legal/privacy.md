# Exhibition privacy notice

## 1. Browsing and test services

Shopify hosts the exhibition and supports its cart and account journeys. The cart, account and checkout can handle information you provide when you test them. Shopify can also receive technical data such as your IP address and browser information. Review how Shopify handles that information in its [consumer privacy policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy/consumers).

## 2. Your browser’s copy

If you save the optional kitchen questionnaire, your hob and cooking preferences, the email you choose to enter and the time of saving are written to this browser. The questionnaire uses those preferences to highlight cookware in the catalogue. The email stays in the browser. Signing up for house emails is a separate form.

The embedded Kansa demo matches questions to a local catalogue and example responses. It can keep up to 40 recent messages in this browser. The catalogue request carries no message text. Please keep personal or sensitive information out of the chat, especially on shared devices.

If you choose to use the microphone, your browser may send audio to its speech recognition service to make a transcript. You can type the same question instead.

## 3. Information you send

The Shopify-hosted contact and email signup forms send the information you submit. Use Contact for a reply about the exhibition. Use the separate signup form only if you want house emails. Checkout testing is for visitors given test details by the operator.

## 4. Choices and questions

Choose **[ Reset Session & Storage ]** in the footer to clear local and session storage for this storefront in this browser, including the questionnaire and Kansa history. Information already sent through Shopify forms or checkout is stored separately. Use your browser settings or any Shopify privacy controls shown for cookie choices.

For privacy questions or requests about information you sent to the exhibition, use [Contact](/pages/contact). The [browser storage page](/pages/cookie-policy) explains what this theme remembers during and between visits.
