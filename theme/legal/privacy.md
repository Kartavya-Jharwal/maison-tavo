- Identity and contact data (name, email, shipping and billing addresses, phone number).
- Order and transaction data (products purchased, payment status, fulfilment details). Payment card data is processed by Shopify and payment providers; we do not intend to store full card numbers.
- Account data if you register (login identifiers, order history, saved addresses, preferences).
- Technical and usage data (IP address, device and browser type, approximate location, pages viewed, referring URLs) via Shopify, cookies, and similar technologies.
- Communications you send us (enquiries, reviews, returns correspondence).
- Marketing preferences (newsletter signup, consent records where required).

## 4. How we use information

- Provide, fulfil, and administer orders, returns, exchanges, and customer service.
- Operate accounts, authentication, and fraud prevention.
- Comply with tax, accounting, consumer, and other legal obligations.
- Improve the storefront, diagnose issues, and understand aggregated shopping behaviour.
- Send service messages (order updates) and, where permitted, marketing about Maison Tavo.
