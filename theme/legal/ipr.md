# Intellectual property

## 1. The work and its makers

The exhibition combines original copy, cookware concepts, images, demonstrations and software with third-party materials. Rights in each contribution remain with its respective rights holder. Credits should travel with the material they describe.

## 2. Traditional techniques

The Chulha studies draw on cookware forms, materials and skills passed between makers. The Maison Tavo name identifies this exhibition. Artisans retain their own names, work and traditions.

## 3. Reuse and permissions

Ask the relevant rights holder before reproducing exhibition photography, drawings or text unless your use is already permitted by law or licence. An asset’s own licence governs its reuse. You can read and compare the catalogue’s machine-readable facts within the exhibition.

## 4. Corrections and rights enquiries

Use [Contact](/pages/contact) to ask about permission or report a rights or attribution concern. Please include the page link, the affected material and a way to reply.
