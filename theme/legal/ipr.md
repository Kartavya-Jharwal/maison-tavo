## 1. Purpose

This notice explains how Maison Tavo and Kartavya (placeholder operator) protect the house’s names, designs, imagery, and storefront, and how visitors may use them.

## 2. Ownership

Unless otherwise stated, all intellectual property in the Maison Tavo house, including trade marks, trade dress, product silhouettes, patterns, lookbook photography, film, copy, graphics, and the selection and arrangement of the storefront, is owned by Maison Tavo, Kartavya, or licensors. Purchase of a garment transfers the physical item, not the underlying design copyright, pattern, or brand rights, except as required by exhaustion or other mandatory law.

## 3. Trade marks

“Maison Tavo”, associated logos, and campaign names are trade marks or trade names, whether or not registered. You may not use them in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation, or that you operate the house. Domain names, social handles, and metadata that imitate the house are not permitted.

## 4. Copyright

Site text, product stories, journal articles, and original photography are protected by copyright. You may view them for personal, non-commercial shopping. You may not reproduce galleries, scrape product data for competing catalogues, train models on house imagery where prohibited, or republish lookbooks without written consent.

## 5. Design and product rights

Maison Tavo products may be protected by design registrations, unregistered design rights, copyright in artistic craftsmanship, and trade dress. Copying, adapting, or manufacturing confusingly similar garments, hardware, or packaging for commercial purposes is prohibited.

## 6. Limited licence to visitors

We grant a limited, revocable, non-transferable licence to access the storefront and to download a copy of a page for personal reference. Framing, mirroring, or systematic extraction of the catalogue is outside this licence.

## 7. User submissions

If you send comments, photos, or reviews, you grant Maison Tavo a worldwide, royalty-free licence to use that content to operate and promote the house, while you retain ownership of your original copyright. Do not submit material you do not have rights to. We may remove content that infringes or violates these terms.

## 8. Third-party rights

Some fonts, stock, or platform components are licensed from third parties (including Shopify). Those licences do not grant you extra rights. Product names of other houses that appear in editorial comparison remain their owners’ property.

## 9. Notice of infringement

If you believe storefront content infringes your IP, send a notice with: (a) your contact details; (b) identification of the work; (c) the URL of the allegedly infringing material; (d) a statement of good-faith belief; and (e) a statement that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised agent. We will consider valid notices and may remove or disable access. Counsel should add a jurisdiction-specific takedown process (for example DMCA) before targeting those markets.

## 10. Enforcement

We may investigate misuse, send cease-and-desist correspondence, use platform reporting tools, and seek injunctions or damages. Nothing in this notice limits remedies available at law.
