### Lead work (use on storefront)

1. **Savi Hotels & Resorts — Operational Lead**, Jaipur, India, Jan 2021–Jul 2026  
   Summary (resume): entrepreneurship-through-acquisition operating control of a leased Savi property within SRBS Group — handover and inventory through demand ramp, **on-property menu design and MICE F&B**, IT estate, commercial playbooks, and a **profitability kill-gate exit when the hold thesis failed**.  
   Published highlights used in theme copy: banquet and meeting spaces; walk-in family lunches sharing the same kitchen and service spine; corporate MICE catering (menu architecture, bill-of-materials, sourcing); rotational pop-up menus; partnered with head chefs on kitchen upkeep **“without claiming a formal hotel line-cook role.”**

2. **Siya The Restaurant, SRBS Group — Menu Engineering Consultant**, Jaipur, Jan 2025–Jan 2026  
   Summary: on-property menu design and back-of-house economics for the Savi/SRBS F&B operation — **191-item architecture**, pricing systems, kitchen controls.  
   Published highlights: OCR of two years of handwritten kitchen registers; FIFO and shift reconciliation; margin-times-velocity matrix; auditable Excel pricing model.

3. **Independent Culinary Development and Kitchen Systems Practice** (resume project)  
   Structured culinary research: mise en place, food safety, fermentation control, FIFO, allergen handling.

4. **Culinary Operations Rotation** (resume project)  
   “Commercial back-of-house and front-of-house rotations across multiple London restaurants and live service environments.”

5. **PACA Applied Food Science** and **PACAKATVA Culinary Data Platform** (resume projects)  
   Small-batch food-science practice; culinary intelligence / third-space concept for chefs.

6. **Danone YoPRO APEX Diagnostic** (resume project; pro bono / modelled advisory — not employment at Danone)  
   Board-level playbook for a precision-nutrition / functional-yoghurt platform. CPG lens.

7. **Siya Menu Design and Restaurant Data Platform** (resume project)  
   End-to-end on-property menu design: taxonomy, composition, BOM, elasticity, manager parser. Work in progress on live site / quick-commerce hooks.

8. **Class 12 — Soma** ([BST/index.html](https://kartavya.tech/Kartavya12B_projects/BST/index.html))  
   Business-studies marketing project “to revolutionize the samosa snack industry.” Live page: “Soma has ascended from a humble marketing project to an upcoming venture.” Do **not** equate Soma with Maison Tavo.

9. **Class 12 — Hospiteri** ([IP project](https://kartavya.tech/Kartavya12B_projects/IP/index.html))
